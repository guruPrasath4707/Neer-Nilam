SCIENTIFIC GUARDRAILS
Modern OSM, HydroRIVERS, recent rainfall, groundwater, crop reports and land cover are modern context, not direct proof of Chola-era conditions. Separate documented fact, archaeological evidence, interpretation, AI-assisted reconstruction and uncertainty. Preserve original files; transformations must have provenance. The data flow remains source -> evidence -> entity -> historical state -> scene pack.

PHYSICAL REPRESENTATION (NOT DECIDED)
Pepper's Ghost: use, if at all, for a constrained narrative overlay or ghosted time-slice; it is not a true volumetric hologram and suffers from viewing-angle/ambient-light constraints.
Projection-mapped physical relief: leading prototype candidate for spatial relationships among terrain, water, settlements and heritage features, subject to calibration, fabrication, ambient light, maintainability and accessibility tests.
Hybrid: consider a projection-mapped relief as the base with an optional transparent reflective/Peppers-Ghost-style story layer only if it improves clarity. Keep software and evidence model presentation-neutral; provide a web/2D accessible mirror. Do not purchase hardware or lock the final display before Judy ma'am's direction and comparative prototyping.
