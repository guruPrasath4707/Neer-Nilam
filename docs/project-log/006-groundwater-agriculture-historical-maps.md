## Acquisition rules

Groundwater and agricultural data are **modern observations**. Keep each well/station's identity, coordinates, date, unit, measurement depth reference, observation method, table and report page. Do not infer ancient groundwater conditions from these modern measurements. Crop report periods and units may differ by publication; do not concatenate them before reviewing definitions.

Historical map assets are not automatically open merely because their catalogue records are searchable. National Archives cartographic access is subject to its rules and registration/reproduction procedures. David Rumsey image rights can vary by map and underlying publisher; commercial publication may require written permission. Store bibliographic metadata first; request only selected records and copies.

## Historical map evidence requirements

For each candidate sheet, preserve:
- archive/catalogue identifier and URL
- map title, creator/surveyor, publisher and stated date
- scale, sheet coverage, legend, north/orientation, and intended use
- original image checksum and the copy/reproduction terms
- georeferencing control points, target coordinate reference system, transform method and residual error
- uncertainty notes, especially where historic map distortion or changing river courses affect placement

A historic map is a dated representation with a purpose and scale, not a perfect ground-truth geometry.
